Monuments of India

Taj Mahal, Agra

  • Location: Agra, Uttar Pradesh
  • Built: 1632–1653
  • Built for: Mumtaz Mahal's tomb

Red Fort, Delhi

  • Location: Old Delhi, Delhi
  • Built: 1639–1648
  • Built for: Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan

Gateway of India, Mumbai

  • Location: Apollo Bunder, Mumbai, Maharashtra
  • Built: 1913–1924
  • Built for: Commemorating the 1911 visit of King George V and Queen Mary to India

Qutub Minar, Delhi

  • Location: Mehrauli, Delhi
  • Built: Started around 1199 AD
  • Built for: Minaret and Islamic architectural complex

Lotus Temple, New Delhi

  • Location: Bahapur, New Delhi
  • Built: 1980–1986
  • Built for: Baha'i House of Worship

Hampi Stone Chariot

  • Location: Vijaya Vittala Temple, Hampi, Karnataka
  • Built: 16th century AD
  • Built for: Shrine dedicated to Garuda, Lord Vishnu's vehicle
